About Me

Hi! I am a software developer with deep roots in backend architecture who also crafts responsive, functional frontends. I believe in pragmatic engineering: shipping complete, production-ready systems over unnecessary complexity.

I lean heavily towards the Majestic Monolith architecture. Keeping business logic, authentication (JWT/RBAC), database operations, and client rendering tightly coupled reduces premature infrastructure overhead and drastically speeds up shipping velocity.

From designing clean RESTful contracts and database schemas to building seamless user interfaces, I focus on end-to-end product delivery that is secure, scalable, and easy to maintain.
